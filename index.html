About Him

Serving the People of Ward 38

📋 Parshad · Ward 38

Anand Agrawal is a dedicated Parshad (Municipal Corporator) representing Swami Atmanand Ward No. 38 in Raipur, Chhattisgarh — one of the most vibrant and growing residential areas of the city.

With a unique background combining a B.Tech in Computer Technology and strong civic experience, Anand Agrawal brings a rare blend of technical expertise and public commitment to his role as a representative.

His approach is simple: listen to residents, act swiftly, and deliver real change. From resolving long-pending civic issues to leading large-scale beautification and infrastructure initiatives, he works tirelessly for the welfare and development of every household in Ward 38.
